Frequently Asked Questions

What are Hymeg HV thick film resistors used for?

The HV series is designed for applications needing higher voltages, wattages, and power densities, with resistance extended to 50,000 Meg Ohms.

What power and voltage ranges are available in the HV series?

Models span about 1–6 W and roughly 1,500–20,000 VDC depending on SKU (HV60 through HV120).

Where can I download HV datasheets?

Each SKU links to a PDF datasheet under /assets/ (for example HV60.pdf). Contact Hymeg for custom requirements.
