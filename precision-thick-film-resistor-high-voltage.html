Frequently Asked Questions

When should I choose HA precision thick film resistors?

Choose the HA series for more precise applications needing higher stability, tighter tolerances, and lower temperature coefficients.

What ranges does the HA series cover?

HA models cover about 0.5–5 W and roughly 750–10,000 VDC, with resistance ranges from tens of kilohms to thousands of megohms depending on SKU.
