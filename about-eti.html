About Hymeg

HYMEG engineers are specialists in precision, high value, high voltage resistors and networks with over 40 years of experience. We are experts in the Thick Film Resistor industry. Our hands-on approach in the manufacture and testing of all of our products, ensures you peace of mind. We strive to provide you, our customer, with quality, custom resistors. We offer you a notably fast, efficient lead time.

Expertise & Experience

HYMEG has more than four decades of experience and a variety of resistor styles including:

• axial leaded power resistors
• voltage dividers
• precision plate resistors
• non-inductive resistors

HYMEG has the capability and experience to provide solutions for all of your resistor needs, with voltage ranges from 1.0 to 30.0 KVDC and average power ratings up to 6 watts. Our products are used in a variety of applications, including:

• medical
• defense
• laser
• instrumentation
• commercial markets




Frequently Asked Questions

What does Hymeg manufacture?

Hymeg manufactures precision, high-value, and high-voltage thick film resistors and resistor networks, including axial leaded power resistors, voltage dividers, precision plate resistors, and non-inductive resistors.

What voltage and power ranges does Hymeg offer?

Hymeg provides solutions with voltage ranges from 1.0 to 30.0 KVDC and average power ratings up to 6 watts.

Which markets use Hymeg resistors?

Hymeg products are used in medical, defense, laser, instrumentation, and commercial markets.
